A personal events tracker for the dates that belong to you and the people you know: holidays, birthdays, namedays, anniversaries, and other milestones — across cultures and calendars.

Screen 1 Dashboard Upcoming mix: your next date, a life bar, and a feed of people, holidays, and milestones. Screen 2 You & places Birthday, years lived, estimated remaining life, home region, places you follow, calendar import. Screen 3 Add someone Follow a person and pull in their holidays, birthday, nameday, and the dates they care about. Screen 4 Add an event Create any life date — not only a holiday. Type, who it belongs to, recurrence, culture.
